# Available upon request: Enhancing data discovery and access to Canadian research

## Overview
In Canada, progress has been limited with respect to supporting the findability and accessibility of restricted data. To comply with the incoming [Tri-agency data deposit requirement](https://science.gc.ca/site/science/en/interagency-research-funding/policies-and-guidelines/research-data-management/proposed-clarifications-data-deposit-requirement-tri-agency-rdm-policy), researchers may be required to make restricted data discoverable and accessible. The objective of the available upon request (AUR) project is to understand the processes, challenges, and opportunities associated with restricted data discovery and access through the lens of Canadian researchers who collect these data, the institutions that support them, and the journals where they publish. 

The AUR project surveys Canadian researchers from 2016 to present (2026) who have indicated in publications that their data is “available upon request”. 

## Project goals
 - To better understand how researchers navigate requests for, and provide access to restricted data;
 - To develop recommendations to improve restricted data discovery and access practices for Canadian researchers; and
 - To inform institutional approaches for improving restricted data guidance, workflows, and infrastructure.
